# Filled-in skill descriptions (2026-06-18)

20 skills in the conversion-psychology suite had placeholder descriptions (`source: community`, dated 2026-04-04). Bodies were complete; only the `description:` trigger line was the scaffold default. Each new description was written from that skill's own body. Review below.

- **awareness-stage-mapper** : Diagnose where an audience sits on the awareness ladder (unaware to most aware) and calibrate message register, sequencing, and persuasion strategy to that stage. Use before writing campaign messaging or offers.
- **brand-perception-psychologist** : Audit what a brand's visual, verbal, and behavioral signals communicate subconsciously, find the gap versus the intended position, and prescribe alignment changes. Use when diagnosing or repositioning how a market perceives a brand.
- **copywriting-psychologist** : Write conversion copy using evidence-based psychological mechanisms (awareness-stage matching, framing, credibility, narrative) to create desire and drive action. Use when copy needs stronger psychological framing or feels generic.
- **customer-psychographic-profiler** : Build a deep psychographic profile of a target customer (desires, fears, identity, worldview, emotional drivers) as a foundation for positioning, copy, and funnel work. Use when demographics alone are not enough.
- **emotional-arc-designer** : Map the deliberate emotional journey a customer should travel across content, an email sequence, a deck, or a product flow, from entry emotion to the state needed for action. Use when content needs an engineered feeling progression.
- **headline-psychologist** : Engineer headlines and titles that capture attention, open a curiosity gap, and signal relevance without becoming clickbait. Use when headlines need stronger stopping power or for testing multiple angles.
- **identity-mirror** : Identify the aspirational identity a customer wants to inhabit and rewrite messaging so the brand or offer reflects that identity back. Use when copy should feel personally resonant and identity-aligned.
- **jobs-to-be-done-analyst** : Uncover the functional, emotional, and social jobs a customer hires a product to do, including switch triggers and competing alternatives. Use when positioning or messaging should be anchored in real customer progress.
- **loss-aversion-designer** : Identify where loss framing outperforms gain framing and apply prospect-theory framing to create credible urgency without fear-mongering. Use when a message should emphasize the cost of inaction.
- **objection-preemptor** : Surface the doubts and resistance a specific customer will feel and neutralize them in advance using inoculation and autonomy-preserving language, without triggering reactance. Use when a funnel or pitch keeps failing on the same objections.
- **onboarding-psychologist** : Design first-use experiences that create early wins, psychological investment, and habit-formation triggers to reduce drop-off. Use when onboarding needs to build momentum and retention.
- **pitch-psychologist** : Structure sales, investor, or product pitches with psychological sequencing that builds desire before the solution and makes the offer feel inevitable. Use when a pitch needs stronger belief progression.
- **price-psychology-strategist** : Apply behavioral economics (anchoring, decoys, tiers, payment framing) to how pricing is structured and presented to improve perceived value and fairness. Use when pricing or offer framing needs better value perception.
- **scarcity-urgency-psychologist** : Engineer genuine, credible scarcity and urgency mechanics that motivate action now without tipping into manipulation or reactance. Use when urgency messaging must feel real and protect trust.
- **sequence-psychologist** : Design email nurture and multi-touch sequences using curiosity loops, reciprocity, commitment, and emotional pacing so each touch prepares the next. Use when a sequence needs a step-by-step persuasion arc.
- **social-proof-architect** : Select, frame, and place the right type of social proof to match a specific audience's trust gap. Use when testimonials, logos, numbers, or case studies need structuring for maximum trust impact.
- **subject-line-psychologist** : Engineer email subject lines and notification copy that earn opens through curiosity, relevance, and pattern-interrupt triggers matched to sequence position. Use when open rates need stronger psychology without losing clarity.
- **trust-calibrator** : Diagnose the specific trust barriers an audience holds and prescribe the exact credibility signals (ability, benevolence, integrity, proof strength) needed. Use when claims feel overstated, underdone, or low-credibility.
- **ux-persuasion-engineer** : Apply choice architecture and behavioral design to UX flows to reduce friction, build commitment, and guide the intended behavior without coercion. Use when conversion friction comes from interaction design, not copy.
- **visual-emotion-engineer** : Map color, typography, spacing, imagery, and layout to specific target emotions and conversion goals. Use when visuals should reinforce a feeling or support persuasion rather than act as decoration.
