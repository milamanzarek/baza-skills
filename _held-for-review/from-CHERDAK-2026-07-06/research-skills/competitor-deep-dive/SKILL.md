\# Competitor Deep-Dive Workflow



1\. \*\*Strategic Extraction:\*\* Scrape the homepage and "About" page. Extract their \*\*Primary Value Proposition\*\* and \*\*Target Audience Segments\*\*.

2\. \*\*Messaging Audit:\*\* Analyze their blog and case studies for "Narrative Pillars." What are the 3 things they want to be known for?

3\. \*\*AI Share of Voice:\*\* Use `Google Search` to find how often this competitor is cited in "Top \[Category] Tools" lists and Reddit "best of" threads.

4\. \*\*Feature Mapping:\*\* List their core product features and identify their "Hook" (the one feature they lead with in ads).

5\. \*\*Output:\*\* Generate a \*\*Competitor Battlecard\*\* including:

&#x20;  - \*\*The "Vibe" Check:\*\* Their brand personality (e.g., "The Safe Enterprise Choice" vs. "The Scrappy Innovator").

&#x20;  - \*\*Vulnerabilities:\*\* Where is their messaging weak or outdated?

&#x20;  - \*\*AI Citation Rank:\*\* How likely is an LLM to recommend them over us?
